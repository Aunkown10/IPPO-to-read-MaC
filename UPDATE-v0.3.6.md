# Ippo to Read v0.3.6

Novel-reader shortcut and appearance-panel patch.

- The EPUB appearance panel no longer opens just because the pointer moves or a chapter changes.
- Press **A** in Novel mode to toggle the appearance/preferences panel.
- **Escape** closes the appearance panel first; press it again to leave the reader.
- **Space** scrolls down roughly one screen in a novel.
- **Shift+Space** scrolls up roughly one screen.
- **Double Space** moves to the next EPUB chapter.
- **Double Shift+Space** moves to the previous EPUB chapter.
- **Alt+Left / Alt+Right** remains available for previous/next chapter.
- **F** remains fullscreen.
- The global Shortcuts dialog now documents the Novel-mode controls.

No database migration is required for this update.
